Lipi dictionary word lists
==========================

Files: ml_dict_words.bin (Malayalam words, typing suggestions), en_dict_words.txt (English words that are never
autocorrected away), and the Malayalam Dictionary the app downloads on first open (lipi-dictionary-v1.db).
Built by tools/dictionary/build.py in the Lipi source repository; the word lists are published in readable form
in tools/dictionary/published/ and next to the dictionary download:
https://lipi-fix.nikhilchappayil.workers.dev/v1/dictionary/ (ml_dict_words.txt, en_dict_words.txt, build.py,
LICENSE.txt).

They are adapted from (both ODbL 1.0, https://olam.in/open/):
- Olam English-Malayalam dictionary by Kailash Nadh.
- Datuk Malayalam-Malayalam dictionary by K. J. Joseph (Datuk), refined and published by Kailash Nadh.
Changes: text normalised (old chillu letters), entries with broken characters removed, meanings ranked by how
common they are and limited per word, long meanings shortened, the English-Malayalam list also read the other
way (Malayalam word -> English words), and word lists made from the headwords.
