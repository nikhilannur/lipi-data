Lipi Malayalam word lists
=========================

Files: ml_words.bin (Malayalam words with frequencies), ml_spellings.bin (Manglish spellings of
Malayalam words), ml_pairs.txt (the words that most often follow each word, for next-word
suggestions) and ml_triples.txt (the words that most often follow each two words). Built by
tools/build_lexicon/build_lexicon.py, build_pairs.py and build_triples.py in the Lipi source repository.

They are adapted from:

1. The Dakshina dataset v1.0 by Google Research (Malayalam Wikipedia text and romanization lexicon),
   CC BY-SA 4.0. https://github.com/google-research-datasets/dakshina
   Roark, Wolf-Sonkin, Kirov, Mielke, Johny, Demirşahin and Hall (2020), "Processing South Asian
   Languages Written in the Latin Script: the Dakshina Dataset", LREC 2020.
   The Wikipedia text is © Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA.

2. FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, Malayalam 2018 list (from OpenSubtitles), content licensed
   CC BY-SA 4.0. https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords

Changes made by Lipi: words counted and merged (subtitle counts weighted to match the Wikipedia
total, ×2), old-style chillu letters converted to atomic chillu, offensive words flagged (not removed), words seen
fewer than ~11 times dropped, only the 200,000 most frequent kept; frequencies rounded to a log
scale; Manglish spellings taken from the Dakshina training split only, lower-cased. ml_pairs.txt: word
pairs counted in the Dakshina Wikipedia training sentences, the 3 most frequent next words (seen at least
3 times) for the 20,000 most frequent words, offensive words left out. ml_triples.txt: the same for two-word
starts (seen at least 3 times), most frequent first, cut at about 1 MB. Both leave out the sentences Lipi uses
as test sets.
