Lipi English word list
======================

File: en_words.bin (English words with frequencies and an offensive-word flag). Built by
tools/build_lexicon/build_english.py in the Lipi source repository. (en_typos.txt next to it is Lipi's own
list of common typos and is not covered by this notice.)

Changes made by Lipi: subtitle words kept only if SCOWL knows them (or a regular -s/-ed/-ing form of
a SCOWL word), words seen fewer than 20 times dropped, contractions and a few app/place names added,
capital letters restored for words like "I" and "India", frequencies rounded to a log scale, offensive
words flagged (not removed).
